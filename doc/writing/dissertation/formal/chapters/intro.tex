\section{Background}

Volcanism at the Earth's surface is a geologically important process that originates due to partial melting of the 
Earth's convecting mantle and subsequent melt segregation. Partial melting generates the oceanic and continental crust and 
determines the large-scale chemical evolution of the Earth. Moreover, similar processes may occur on other terrestrial planets. 
Most of the Earth's mantle is generally below its melting point, while partial melting occurs in the following 
three tectonic settings.

\vspace*{-6pt}\begin{description}  
 \item[\ \ Mid-ocean ridges:] Upwelling of the convecting mantle beneath mid-ocean ridges leads to
   decompression melting and generates the oceanic crust.
 \item[\ \ Hot-spots:] Thermal anomalies induce melting beneath hot-spots and generate ocean islands
   and oceanic plateaus as well as flood basalt provinces on the continents.
 \item[\ \ Subduction zones:] The addition of fluids that lower the melting point induces
   flux-melting above the dehydrating down-going oceanic crust and generates the continental crust.
\end{description}\vspace*{-6pt}

\section{Notable Existing Software}

We introduce some existing software the solves similar problem but different from what we did.

ASPECT : One of the most notable and advanced solver with excellent mesh control and numerics.
It follows the discussion in~\cite{SchubertTurcotteOlson2001} and solves a Stokes system describing the movement of solid mantle 
material coupled with a advection-diffusion-reaction
equation system transporting energy and chemical species.
Phase change is included as a right hand side of the transport equation as a part of the reaction term.
``However, this will not consider melt extraction or any relative movement between melt and solid 
and there might be problems where the transport of melt is of interest".\cite{Bangerth2018}, where
it is the main focus of our solver.

TerraFERMA : A high level wrapper for FEniCS,  PETSc and a science neural options handling system SPuD. The main
focus of the code is to provide a rapid and reproducible construction of multi-physics model arising in geophysics 
scenarios.

pMELTS: Thermodynamics simulation of melting and freezing. Mechanical behaviors and transport not emphasized.
